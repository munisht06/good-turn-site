Common questions

I did not get my sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder for an email from Good Turn. Codes work for one hour. You can ask for a new one from the same screen after a minute.

How do I join my family?

Ask a family admin for the six-character family code. On the first screen choose "I have a family code", sign in with your email, then type the code and your name.

I got a new phone

Install Good Turn and sign in with the same email address. Your family and chores are waiting for you.

How do I stop notifications?

On an iPhone, open Settings, find Good Turn, and turn notifications off. In the web app on Android, press and hold the Good Turn icon, tap App info, then Notifications. The app still works without them.
